Vehicle

Sets the car's configuration the head unit reads at start-up: brand, Performance Pages pictures and gauges, Hellcat red/black key and supercharger pages, launch dial, and the theme and boot splash for that car. A manual gearbox adds the shift light.

Theme & splash

The list shows the themes the head unit itself offers for this car (an SRT car gets the SRT themes, the Viper its own, other cars their brand's). Pick another car above for other themes. The splash shows on the next boot.

Language

The market decides which languages Settings › Display › Language offers (changing it restarts the head unit). Picking a language switches it right away.
